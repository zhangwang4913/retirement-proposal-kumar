You've just turned 56 and reached the CPF Full Retirement Sum (FRS) — a meaningful milestone. The question now is what to do with the next lump sum decision: top up further into CPF LIFE for guaranteed lifetime income, or redeploy the same capital outside CPF to build growth and income while keeping your money accessible. This proposal lays out both paths side by side, using your retirement model and the actual product illustrations, so you can decide with your eyes open.

5The Deferral Option — Delaying CPF LIFE to Age 70
CPF LIFE payouts increase by approximately 6–7% for each year they are deferred past 65, up to age 70 — meaning a payout deferred to 70 could be roughly 24–30% higher than one started at 65. This is valuable for you, but only if you can comfortably bridge the income gap between 65 and 70 without deferral. Strategy A's income is CPF LIFE alone, so deferring means going without that income entirely for 5 years. Strategy B's dividend income (~$19,385/yr) and cash reserves can bridge that gap comfortably, making deferral a realistic option rather than a hardship — giving you the choice, not just the theory.
